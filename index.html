My Bio

My name is Tiana, I'm from Madagascar — a Full Stack Developer with a passion for automation and problem-solving.

Four years ago, I started doing tasks on the Internet across several platforms to earn some income. After a while, I began wondering: "what if I automated these tasks?"

That's when I started researching and learning how to automate things in browsers and on Android phones. I realized that being a developer was making my life much easier, so I became deeply interested in it — and that's what drove me toward becoming a Full Stack Developer.

In 2025, I earned my Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering, and I am now a Master's student at CNTEMAD. During that journey, I also learned to use AI, because the way of building things changed completely once it arrived.

I have completed more than 10 projects that I consider serious, professional work since becoming a developer. You can explore them in the Projects section of my portfolio website.
